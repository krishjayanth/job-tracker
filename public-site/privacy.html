Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version
  • nuj has read-only access to Gmail and only reads emails that look like job application updates.
  • It keeps a summary of each application email (company, role, stage, subject, sender, date). It doesn't keep full email bodies.
  • If you add a Groq API key, part of each matching email is sent to Groq under your key to work out what it means. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or third-party analytics.
  • Each person's data is kept separate. You can disconnect Gmail or delete your account from the app at any time.

Who we are

nuj ("the app", "we") is a personal, open-source job application tracker created by Krish Jayanth. This policy covers the hosted copy of the app and explains what happens to your information when you sign in and connect Gmail. If you run your own copy from the source code, you operate it and this policy doesn't cover your copy.

What we collect

Your Google account

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, Google's ID for your account, and whether Google has verified the email. We use them only to check that your account is allowed to use the app, to keep you signed in, and to keep your data separate from everyone else's.

Gmail data

If you connect Gmail, the app asks Google for read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission). It can't send, delete, label or change any email. Every few minutes it runs a Gmail search that matches emails from hiring platforms (such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever and HackerRank) or with subjects about applications, assessments, interviews and offers. Only emails matching that search are opened. For each one it reads the sender, subject, date and message text.

From each email it keeps:

  • the company, role, job ID and stage it found (for example "Interview");
  • the email's subject, sender, date and Gmail's short preview snippet;
  • any deadline found in the email;
  • a one-line summary of the email and, if Groq read it, a short explanation of how it was read;
  • your answers when you confirm or move an email to a different application;
  • Gmail's internal message and thread IDs, so the same email isn't processed twice.

The full text of the email isn't stored.

To keep syncing, the app stores the access and refresh tokens Google issues for your account.

Your Groq API key

If you add a Groq API key, it's stored encrypted and used only to send your own emails to Groq as described below. It's never shown back to you in full (only its last four characters), and you can remove it in the app's Settings at any time.

What you enter

Applications you add by hand, notes, links, deadlines and any edits you make are stored with the rest of your applications.

nuj also counts how often you fix its sorting, as totals only (for example "3 emails moved, 1 sender ignored"), so we can see whether sorting is getting better. If you ignore a sender, its domain (such as example.com) is kept so nuj can skip it.

Invite requests

If you ask for an invite on the home page, we store your name, email address, where you are in your job search and anything you write, so we can decide on and send your invite. Only the person who runs nuj sees it. Your IP address is used briefly to stop the form being sent over and over, and isn't stored. To have your request deleted, contact us.

Problem reports

If you send a report with "Report a problem", we store what you wrote, your email address, and technical details to help fix it: the page you were on, your browser, recent error messages, and how emails are being sorted. If you report a problem with one application and tick the box to include it, its company, role, status and timeline (stages, dates and email subjects) are attached too. Only the person who runs nuj reads reports.

Keeping the app working

So the person who runs nuj can spot and fix problems, it keeps a few facts about each account: when you joined, when you last used the app, whether Gmail is connected, how your emails are sorted, how many applications and emails you have, and when your last sync ran. It also keeps a log of errors from syncing your account and from the app in your browser: the error message (with email addresses removed), roughly where it happened, and when. Neither includes your applications, companies, roles or email contents. The log is capped, and deleting your account removes you from it.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie: a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It isn't used for tracking. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

How it's used

Your information is used only to provide the app's features to you: finding your application emails, working out each application's status and deadlines, and showing them on your dashboard and in exports you request. It isn't used for advertising, profiling, or anything unrelated to tracking your applications.

Who it's shared with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that run the app, and only as much as each needs:

  • Groq (AI processing), only if you've added a Groq API key. For each matching email, the sender, date, subject and up to the first 3,500 characters of the message text are sent to Groq's API using your key, which returns the company, role, job ID, stage, any deadline, a short summary and an explanation. If it isn't clear whether an email is about a job at all, the sender, subject and a shorter excerpt are sent first to check, and an email that isn't about a job goes no further. If you have several applications at the same company and an email doesn't say which one it's about, the roles, job IDs, statuses, application dates and recent email subjects of those applications are sent with it so Groq can pick the right one. Without a key, or if Groq is unavailable, the app uses built-in rules instead and nothing is sent.
  • Render (hosting). The app runs on Render's servers.
  • Neon (database). Your applications, the email details listed above, your Google tokens and your encrypted Groq key are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon.
  • Google, to sign you in and to read Gmail as described above.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the security of the app and its users.

Google user data

nuj's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Gmail data is used only to provide and improve the job-tracking features you can see in the app.
  • It's transferred to others only as described in Who it's shared with, to provide those features, for security, or as required by law.
  • It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your emails unless you ask us to (for example, by choosing to include an application's timeline in a problem report), it's needed for security, or the law requires it.

Security

Connections to the app use HTTPS. Access is limited to the Google accounts the app is set up for, each person can only see their own data, stored Groq keys are encrypted, and session cookies are signed and can't be read by scripts in the page. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users about any breach as the law requires.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you use the app. You're in control of it:

  • Delete an application from its details panel. It's removed, and its emails won't be added again.
  • Disconnect Gmail in the app's Settings. Your stored Google tokens are deleted, the app's Gmail access is cancelled at Google, and it stops reading your inbox.
  • Remove access from Google's side at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete everything with "Delete my account" in the app's Settings. Your applications, email details, Google tokens, Groq key and any problem reports you sent are deleted straight away, and the app's Gmail access is cancelled at Google. Copies in the database provider's backups expire on their own schedule. You can also contact us and we'll delete all data stored for your account within 30 days.

Data held by Groq is governed by Groq's own policies.

Children

The app isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how Gmail data is used, we'll ask for your consent before it applies to you.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/krishjayanth/job-tracker/issues. Please don't include personal details in a public issue; ask for a private way to follow up instead.
